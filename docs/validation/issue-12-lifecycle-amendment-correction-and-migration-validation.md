## Validation Layers

### Structural validation

Draft 2020-12 JSON Schema validates:

* immutable path-matching `$id` values;
* closed envelopes and branches;
* required properties;
* identifier and timestamp syntax;
* controlled vocabularies;
* exact versioned reference shapes;
* reason and evidence conditionals;
* projection category/code compatibility;
* and prohibited canonical fields on the integrity-finding projection.

### Application validation

Application validation remains responsible for:

* exact authoritative resolution and authorization;
* storage-path and envelope agreement;
* lifecycle-transition legality and history reconciliation;
* amendment materiality and target-revision preconditions;
* successor, consolidation, dependency, migration, and ownership graphs;
* no silent successor following or reference retargeting;
* semantic preservation during migration;
* destination and child reconciliation during ownership correction;
* exceptional-removal authorization and successful payload and derived-data disposition;
* deterministic integrity-finding generation and conservative indeterminate handling;
* and coordinated atomic or recoverable persistence.
